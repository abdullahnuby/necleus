Scientific interpretation:
- Richer temporal representations generally improve with more examples; the v0.29 four-window representation plateaus earlier than full spike rasters and 8/16-bin features.
- A supervised GBM trained on raw input approaches 99% at 6000 examples, but is near chance at 200; this is evidence of sample/capacity effects on this synthetic distribution, not evidence of STDP efficiency.
- The v0.30 scikit-learn logistic baseline (standardized LBFGS) differs from v0.29's custom minibatch logistic baseline, so absolute differences between versions must not be attributed to feature representation alone.
- This is a decodability/sample-efficiency study, not a continual-learning/forgetting comparison and not an STDP-rule comparison.
